#TinyTerrain is a simple python game which built for TI-Nspire CX II and TI-Nspire CX II CAS,you can download it to your handhold by:
    1.Download my release.
    2.Connect your handhold to your computer.
    3.Open TI-Nspire Computer APP;
    4.Click File.
    5.Download TinyTerrain.tns to your handhold.
    6.Run it in your Document-2 Browse(in Calculator)
